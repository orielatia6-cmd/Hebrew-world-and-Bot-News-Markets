---
name: Yahoo Finance RSS quality
description: Source attribution and ranking guidance for Yahoo Finance market feeds.
---

Yahoo Finance market RSS feeds were available and supplied recent entries with publication dates and HTTPS article links, but the entries did not include separate publisher metadata.

**Why:** The feed can add useful market coverage, but it should not be treated as equivalent to a primary newswire or official source when the original publisher is not identified.

**How to apply:** Keep Yahoo Finance as a secondary source, retain the Yahoo Finance attribution, and continue rejecting items with missing or unreliable dates and links.
